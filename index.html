Check the official source before you apply

Fees, visa amounts and deadlines change every year. Our guides explain how everything works, but always confirm the final numbers on the official programme or government page before you pay anything or book a flight.

  • Erasmus Mundus Catalogue — every EU-funded joint master’s, direct from the European Commission’s agency (EACEA).
  • Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters — student information — who can apply and how the scholarship works.
  • Study at the University of Oslo — an example of checking tuition and admission rules on a university’s own official pages.
  • DAAD Scholarship Database — every DAAD funding programme for international students, with current rates and deadlines.
  • uni-assist — the central application service for around 180 German universities; check whether your programme applies through it.
  • UDI — Norway study permit — the Norwegian Directorate of Immigration’s requirements and the current proof-of-funds amount.
  • Studyinfo — applying in Finland — Finland’s official application portal, plus Migri for the student residence permit rules.
  • EURES — European Job Mobility Portal — the EU’s official portal for jobs and living and working conditions across Europe.
